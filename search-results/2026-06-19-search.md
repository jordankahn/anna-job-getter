### Why (structural limitation)
Niche boards (npo.net, Idealist, League of Chicago Theatres, Foundation List,
Chicago Artists Coalition, Chicago Cultural Alliance, Americans for the Arts,
Workable) block automated fetch with HTTP 403, so sourcing falls back to
WebSearch, whose index lags the live boards by days to weeks. Fresh in-window
posts exist on the live boards (e.g., the Grant Park Music Festival GM role Anna
found was posted the same day) but are not yet search-indexed. More automated
scanning will not clear this ceiling.

### Recommended operating model
1. Check the live "sort by newest" board links below in a browser (no 403 there).
2. Paste promising links into the **My Job Links** sheet.
3. Run "check the sheet" — each gets a tailored package. (This is working: the
   After School Matters, City Colleges, and Grant Park packages all came this way.)
