# Niche Job Sources — Chicago Arts / Nonprofit / Arts Education

Specialized boards and org career pages that surface roles the generic
LinkedIn/Indeed feed misses. Work these first. (Standing pre-filtered search URLs
are added by the search workflow as they are verified.)

## Arts administration / cultural sector
- **Arts Alliance Illinois** — statewide arts job board.
- **Chicago Artists Coalition (CAC)** — arts jobs + opportunities.
- **League of Chicago Theatres** — theater jobs board (admin + production).
- **ArtSearch (Theatre Communications Group)** — national performing-arts jobs.
- **Americans for the Arts** Job Bank — arts admin nationwide, filter Chicago/remote.
- **American Alliance of Museums (AAM)** JobHQ — museum roles.
- **Association of Performing Arts Professionals (APAP)** career center.
- **Ingenuity Inc.** (Chicago arts education) — partner org listings.
- **Arts Work Fund / Illinois Humanities** — sector postings.

## Nonprofit sector (Chicago-heavy)
- **Idealist** — nonprofit jobs, filter Chicago + remote + salary.
- **Work for Good** (formerly DonorSearch jobs) — nonprofit roles.
- **Chicago Jobs Council** member listings.
- **Forefront IL** (statewide nonprofit network) job board.
- **Axelson Center (North Park University)** nonprofit job board.
- **Koya Partners / Diversified Search** — mission-driven placements.
- **NonprofitJobs / Greenlights / Work in Nonprofits**.

## Arts education / teaching-artist orgs (direct career pages)
- **Old Town School of Folk Music**
- **Merit School of Music**
- **The People's Music School**
- **Urban Gateways**
- **Snow City Arts**
- **Chicago Children's Theatre**, **Lookingglass**, **Steppenwolf** (education depts)
- **Lyric Opera of Chicago** (education/community programs)
- **Chicago Symphony Orchestra / Negaunee Music Institute**
- **JCCs**, **park districts** cultural programs, **Chicago Public Library** programs

## Employers worth watching directly
Museums (Art Institute, MCA, Field, Shedd, Adler, MSI, Chicago History Museum),
major theaters (Goodman, Steppenwolf, Chicago Shakespeare, Writers, Court),
foundations (Terra, Driehaus, MacArthur), universities (Northwestern, UChicago,
DePaul, Columbia College, Roosevelt) arts/education departments, and large
cultural nonprofits (JCC Chicago, After School Matters, Gallery 37/Ingenuity).

## Search tips
- Prefer board filters for "$50,000+", "Chicago / hybrid", and "remote".
- Set up email alerts on Idealist, ArtSearch, and Arts Alliance Illinois.
- Check org career pages weekly; many never cross-post to aggregators.
